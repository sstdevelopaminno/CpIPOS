# RW transaction and Finance integration — draft, not live

The isolated TypeScript functions prepare validated procurement quantities, weighted carrying value, credit limits/settlements and balanced journal previews. They do not authorize, persist, move inventory, verify payments, or post accounting records. This draft is intentionally not a production release.

## Production schema observations (2026-11-27)

- Existing orders, order_items, payments, products, ingredients, recipes and stock_movements are tenant/branch scoped. Product IDs are globally unique but the existing product SKU uniqueness is (tenant_id, branch_id, sku); verify scope at all new references.
- Existing Finance tables: finance_accounting_documents, finance_accounting_document_lines, finance_accounting_journals, finance_accounting_journal_lines, finance_accounting_allocations and finance_accounting_chart. Do not introduce a competing ledger.
- The current document tax constraint permits nonzero tax only for verified Recycling VAT source snapshots. RW requires its own reviewed additive tax-source migration; do not pretend to be Recycling. Journal event_type currently permits document_issued only and document_id is unique in each tenant/branch. Reversal must use approved linked reversal documents or a reviewed versioned event change; do not delete or overwrite posted journals.

## Persistence and transaction contracts to implement next

1. Versioned RW unit/barcode and tier catalog isolated from SD. Exact product + tenant + branch, unique barcode across active product unit aliases and existing SKU, optimistic catalog version, authorized owner/manager, audit. Server reads authoritative tier/stock; submitted client price is only an assertion. Preserve original SD prices and stock recipes. Start with THB integer satang and integer base units; do not silently coerce weights or other currencies.
2. Supplier PO lines and goods receipt lines, immutable item/unit/cost snapshots, cumulative received quantities locked and bounded by PO quantity. Stock ledger source keys unique per receipt line, no cash payment at goods receipt. Receipt records stock value, supplier bill clears goods-received-not-invoiced and establishes AP. Credit notes and returns reverse sources once, with appropriate documents.
3. Sales checkout captures verified price revision and customer terms; one canonical order and one idempotency key. Lock product/stock/customer/credit in stable order. Sale, COGS, receivable or verified settlement, audit, and canonical Finance document/journal must be atomic. Reject insufficient stock, stale prices, currency mismatch, duplicate or unverified transfer and excess credit. No client-supplied ledger account UUIDs; resolve mapped account roles against tenant chart.
4. Credit settlement uses locked outstanding balance and evidence, unique reference/idempotency; no sale duplication or overpayment. AR/AP aging uses issued and allocated canonical documents. Respect locked accounting periods, roles, document dates, retention and tax-source verification. No automatic tax invoice without verified issuer/customer details and lawful tax status.
5. UAT: same idempotency key and payload returns existing result; different payload rejects; injected failure rolls back sale/stock/finance together; concurrent receipts/credit and overselling; cross-tenant/branch denial; partial receiving and settlement; cancellations/reversals; VAT reports; legacy SD and other modes unaffected. Use synthetic staging data only, then authenticated read-only customer verification.
